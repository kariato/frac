# FRAC v0.1.0 — browser preview

A spatial falling-block puzzle inspired by Frac (1990), built for keyboard and touch.

- Geometric blocks: short bars; flat 2×2, 3×3, 4×4 squares; solid 2×2×2 and 3×3×3 cubes; a 2×2×3 block. Oversize pieces are omitted where necessary.
- Size-weighted piece bags: about 91% small pieces, with progressively rarer large blocks (3×3×3 about once per 300 pieces).
- New games fall at 2× speed. Legacy saves keep their original timing and piece catalogs.
- Six well presets and configurable width, depth, and height.
- Marathon and Practice; hold, next previews, landing ghost, and Practice undo.
- Rotatable 3D well with four stable views and view-relative controls.
- Visible touch controls, phone/tablet layouts, remappable keyboard controls.
- Interactive tutorial, column-height inset, Practice layer inspection.
- Local snapshots and records, backup import/export, deterministic replay export.
- Reduced motion, high contrast, procedural sound, and optional mathematical background.
- GPL-3.0-only source, GitHub Pages hosting, and itch-ready HTML5 ZIP.

**Prerelease limitations:** physical iPhone/iPad/Android playtesting and actual itch.io publication remain pending. Automated mobile emulation is not physical-device verification. No gamepad, Sprint, Time Attack, online leaderboard, or offline installation yet. The 3D board does not have a nonvisual play mode.

Upload the HTML5 archive to itch.io as an HTML Game. The source archive corresponds to this release commit. Progress on Pages and itch.io is separate; use Settings → backup export/import to transfer it.
